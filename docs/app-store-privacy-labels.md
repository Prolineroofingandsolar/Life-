# App Store Connect — privacy answers

The exact answers to enter under **App Store Connect ▸ App Privacy**, and the
notes to paste into **App Review Information**.

These must agree with `Life/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`, `LifeTasksWidget/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`
and `docs/privacy-policy.md`. The three are authored in different places, and a
disagreement between them is a rejection that costs a full review cycle — so if
you change one, change all three.

---

## Data collection

**Does this app collect data?** → **Yes**

Everything below is **linked to the user's identity** (it is stored against a
Firebase account) and **not used for tracking** — Life has no analytics, no
advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs that profile anyone. Answer
**No** to every tracking question.

| Category | Type | Collected | Purpose | Linked | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health & Fitness | **Health** | Yes | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Health & Fitness | **Fitness** | Yes | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Contact Info | **Email Address** | Yes | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Contact Info | **Name** | Yes | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Identifiers | **User ID** | Yes | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| User Content | **Other User Content** | Yes | App Functionality | Yes | No |

Why each one:

- **Health** — the 20 HealthKit types Life reads: sleep, heart rate, resting
  heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, VO2 max, wrist temperature,
  body mass, body fat, lean mass and BMI.
- **Fitness** — steps, active energy, exercise time, distance, flights climbed,
  and the workouts logged in the app.
- **Email Address** — received from Sign in with Apple. Often an Apple private
  relay address, but declare it regardless.
- **Name** — optional, entered in Settings, and included in the synced snapshot.
- **User ID** — the Firebase account identifier that the synced data is stored
  against.
- **Other User Content** — tasks, habits, routines, notes and direct debits, all
  part of the synced snapshot.

### Do NOT declare

- **Precise or Coarse Location** — the world map and all location capability
  were removed before v1. If that feature ever comes back, this table changes
  with it.
- **Photos or Videos** — progress photos never leave the device, and the
  equipment-scanner image is used once for identification and not retained. If
  you would rather be conservative you may declare Photos with purpose *App
  Functionality*; do not declare it as linked to identity.
- **Purchases, Browsing History, Search History, Sensitive Info, Contacts,
  Diagnostics, Usage Data** — none are collected.

## Other answers

- **Privacy policy URL** — the published URL of `docs/privacy-policy.md`. Must
  be live at submission and stay live.
- **Does the app use encryption?** → the app uses only standard HTTPS, which is
  exempt. `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false` is set in `Life/Info.plist`,
  so this should not be asked per-build.
- **Age rating** — the questionnaire asks about *Medical or Treatment
  Information*. The coach gives fitness and recovery guidance derived from
  health data, so answer honestly rather than optimistically; expect 12+.
- **Account deletion** — required (guideline 5.1.1(v)). Point to
  **Settings ▸ Delete Account**.

## App Review notes

Paste into **App Review Information ▸ Notes**, after filling in the demo
account. A reviewer signing into an empty account sees an empty app and rejects
it for insufficient content, so the demo account must have real data in it —
several weeks of workouts, habits with streaks, tasks, and health figures.

> **Demo account:** [APPLE ID / EMAIL] / [PASSWORD]
> Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method. The account above is already
> populated with sample tasks, habits, workouts and health history.
>
> Life can also be used entirely signed-out — skip the sign-in screen to see the
> app with local data only.
>
> **HealthKit.** Life reads Apple Health to show sleep, heart rate, recovery and
> activity alongside workouts and habits. It never writes to Apple Health. On
> the Simulator there is no Health data, so the Health tab will show empty
> states; the app is fully functional without it, and the demo account carries
> its own recorded figures so the features can be seen.
>
> **The AI coach.** The coach card on Today, and the Ask Coach screen, produce
> guidance generated by Google Gemini through our own Cloud Functions proxy. It
> is off by default and requires an explicit consent screen listing what is
> sent. Only derived figures are transmitted — sleep duration against the user's
> own baseline, recovery expressed in words, step counts, and counts of
> outstanding tasks. No name, no email, no raw health records, and no task or
> habit titles unless separately enabled. We use Gemini's paid tier, which does
> not train on submitted content.
>
> The coach is **explicitly not medical advice** and does not diagnose or treat.
> It cannot prescribe, and responses mentioning medication, doses, supplements
> or purchases are rejected server-side before reaching the app. With AI turned
> off, the same card runs on rules inside the app and nothing is transmitted.
>
> **Camera.** Used only for the optional gym-equipment scanner: one photo is
> sent to be identified and is not retained anywhere. Off unless enabled.
>
> **Widgets.** Tasks, habits and a coach tip, plus a Live Activity during a
> workout. Add them from the Home Screen gallery.
>
> **Account deletion** is at Settings ▸ Delete Account. It removes the Firebase
> account and the data stored against it.
